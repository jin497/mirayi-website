Game Animation & Art Services

Mirayi brings decades of high-end broadcast animation experience directly into the game development pipeline.

We are a 50-person studio bridging the gap between narrative artistry and real-time technical requirements. Whether you need emotional in-engine cutscenes, responsive gameplay cycles, or optimized character rigs, our team delivers assets that are story-driven and engine-ready.

Cinematics & Cutscenes

We translate complex narratives into compelling visual experiences. We handle the full spectrum of production, from pre-rendered marketing trailers to real-time, in-engine story sequences (Unreal/Unity), ensuring high-fidelity acting and emotional impact.

Gameplay Animation

Animation that feels good to play. We create grounded, impactful motion for player characters, enemies, and creatures. Our focus is on weight, timing, and readability for combat actions, traversal cycles, and interactive reactions.

Assets & Rigging

Technical foundation meets artistic vision. We provide clean, optimized mesh topology suitable for modern game engines. Our technical team builds robust, animator-friendly control rigs designed for complex performances and efficient pipelines.
