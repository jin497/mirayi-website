Humble beginnings

In 2005, Mirayi Animation Studio embarked on a new frontier in the animation world. Our vision is to be a world's renowned creative content creator, who inspires and bring joy to people. Now, we serve as one of the leading content animation studios in Malaysia.

Animation maker and more

Being a pioneer in the Malaysian Animation industry, Mirayi also plays a significant role as a point of contact for international producers and animation companies seeking information on co-production and work-for-hire options in Malaysia.

Mirayi Animation Studio has been awarded MSC Malaysia status and is fully licensed by the National Film Development Corporation of Malaysia (FINAS).
